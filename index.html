FAQ

Common questions before the first call

How long until we see value?

The insurance ontology arrives fully built, so there is no model-training project. First insights appear during the pilot, on your own policies and claims.

Does this replace Guidewire, Duck Creek or our other core systems?

No. Qubru reads from the systems you already run. Documents and data stay where they are; Qubru connects the reasoning across them.

What data does Qubru read?

Structured records from your cores, CRM and warehouses, plus unstructured content: PDFs, scans, emails, and the free text already living inside your core systems, like adjuster narratives and underwriting memos.

How does Qubru help our AI strategy?

It gives every AI agent the same governed memory: full history, insurance semantics and traceable sources. Your AI projects start from trusted data instead of rebuilding context each time.

What happens to our underwriting and claims teams?

They keep the decision. Qubru surfaces the pattern and the evidence behind it; your people review and decide.

How do you handle security and access?

Tenant isolation, least-privilege access and a tamper-evident audit trail on every action, built in rather than bolted on.
