The product

From document chaos to governed knowledge

One pipeline: ingest any document, understand it, connect it, and govern it — with the controls a regulated carrier actually needs.

📄

Document understanding

Ingest PDFs and scans, then classify and extract the entities that matter — parties, policies, claims, dates, amounts — with the model you choose.

🕸️

Entity & relationship graph

Every document resolves into a connected graph, unifying the same party or policy across files so you can actually trace what relates to what.

🛡️

Retention & disposition

Encode retention rules once and let the system surface what's due for review or disposition — turning policy into enforceable workflow.

🔒

Audit & access control

Tenant isolation, least-privilege access, and a tamper-evident audit trail on every action — designed for data-residency-sensitive regimes.

How it works

Four steps, one governed system of record

Ingest

Drop in documents from your existing stores. Qubru handles extraction, including scanned and image-based files.

Classify

Each document is triaged and labelled, so the right retention and handling rules apply automatically.

Connect

Entities are extracted and unified into a cross-document graph you can explore and query.

Govern

Retention, disposition, access, and audit run continuously — with every action recorded.
